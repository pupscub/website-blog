---
title: "Why I Code"
date: "2024-01-05"
excerpt: "Reflecting on what drives me to build software and solve problems through technology."
---

I started coding because it felt like a superpower — the ability to bring an idea to life in front of you. Years later,
the spark is still the same. I write about the mentors who nudged me forward, the communities that shaped my craft, and
the responsibility that comes with building tools people rely on every day.

I keep investing in skills that compound: structured thinking, clear writing, and empathy for users. Those muscles make
technical work durable and ensure that the systems I design stay grounded in the problems they solve.
