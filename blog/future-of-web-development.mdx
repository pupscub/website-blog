---
title: "The Future of Web Development"
date: "2023-12-20"
excerpt: "Predictions and thoughts on where web technology is heading in the next few years."
---

The web keeps reinventing itself. From AI-assisted workflows to edge-native data storage, the stack is changing faster
than ever. I'm watching three themes closely:

1. **Ambient AI tooling** that gives teams copilots for everything from data cleaning to accessibility reviews.
2. **Composable architectures** powered by streaming and server components, which make it easier to mix static and
   dynamic experiences.
3. **Team-friendly DX** where infrastructure scaffolds the happy path so product engineers can stay close to users.

Whatever the future holds, grounding decisions in user outcomes never goes out of style.
